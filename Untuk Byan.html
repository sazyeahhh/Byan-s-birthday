Dear, the Son of February

(Untuk segala baik rupa dunia yang aku sayangi; yang senyum dan tawanya dilimpahi puja-puji; yang hadirnya jadi pelebur sendu; selamat mengulangi hari lahir!)

Before we dive in, let’s do a quick recap—because knowing you, you might’ve already forgotten some of this.

It felt familiar but different—like picking up an old book and realizing you still remember the story. Oh, and this was back when you still thought I was that freak.

This was when you started joking around, and honestly? It felt nice.

You didn’t have to help, but you did. And not just once. You’ve always been there, quietly supportive in a way that doesn’t ask for anything in return. I noticed, by the way. I always do.

Stuck in the rain, playing Uno, being unnecessarily loud—who knew something that simple could be so fun? But did we get annoyed? Nah, we just went with it and had fun.

You were working on your project, and I was just there, keeping you company. Nothing big, nothing dramatic—just being there, I hope that matters to you.

A special letter for my 3-month friend—the one who’s effortlessly cool.
